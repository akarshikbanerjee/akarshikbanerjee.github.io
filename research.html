May the Phone Be With You: Impact of Smartphone Ownership on Women's Empowerment
Access and capability are usually bundled: women who get a phone are often also the ones who get trained to use it. A randomized trial in Uttar Pradesh separates the two, and asks a harder follow-up question — where empowerment measures move, is that expanded information and outside options, or is it the measure picking up expressed attitudes rather than realized bargaining power?†
